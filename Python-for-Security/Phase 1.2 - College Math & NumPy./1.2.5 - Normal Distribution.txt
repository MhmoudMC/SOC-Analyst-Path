(SOC Ball Curve)
* Concept : Most hours generate a baseline level of events (e.g 50 logs/hour). Standard deviation measures normal noise.
* 3-Sigma Rule: 68% of normal activity falls within ~1Sigma, 95% within ~2Sigma, and 99.7% within ~3Sigma, anything beyond ~3Sigma is statistical anomaly.
* Metric: The Z-score measures exactly how many standard deviations an event is from mean : (x - mule) / sigma . where x is the current traffic, mule is the baseline mean, and sigma is standard deviation.

import numpy as np
from scipy.stats import norm

# Baseline trafafic parameters
mean_traffic = 50
std_traffic = 15

# Probability density of normal vs spike traffic 
prob_normal = norm.pdf(50, loc=mean_traffic, scale=std_traffic)
prob_spike = norm.pdf(450, loc=mean_traffic, scale=std_traffic)
print(f"Probability of 50 logs: {prob_normal:.4f}")
print(f"Probability of 450 logs: {prob_spike:.10f}") # Practically 0%
