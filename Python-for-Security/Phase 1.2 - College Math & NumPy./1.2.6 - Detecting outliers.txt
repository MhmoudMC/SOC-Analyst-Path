# The 3-Sigma Rule
	* Mean (mule): Your normal average traffic level.
	* StdDev(Sigma): How much traffic naturally fluctuates during normal hours.
	* 3 StdDev Threshold: In a stand normal distribution, 99.7% of all normal activity stays within 3 standard deviations from the average. Anything above this threshold triggers alert.
